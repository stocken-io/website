How STKN works

STKN is not speculative — it has real, immediate utility inside any integrated platform from day one.

💳
Pay with STKN

Use STKN to pay for subscriptions across integrated platforms, with payment terms defined independently by each platform.

🎁
Earn STKN

Platforms reward users with STKN for genuine engagement and meaningful actions. Each platform defines its own reward logic independently.

🔥
Deflationary Burns

5% of every STKN payment is burned each quarter. Less supply over time, fully transparent on-chain. Burn events are publicly announced.

🌐
Open Ecosystem

Any authorized fintech platform can integrate STKN via the Partner API. Each partner defines their own reward amounts and payment terms independently.
